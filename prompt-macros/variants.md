# VARIANTS

## Purpose

Generate multiple distinct approaches before committing to one.

## Trigger

- `VARIANTS: [task]`
- `VARIANTS[n]: [task]`

## Behavior

1. Generate 3-5 options with genuinely different angles.
2. For each option, note the method, strengths, and limitations.
3. Compare them against the real goal.
4. Recommend one or two.

## Good for

- naming and framing decisions
- architecture choices
- content angles
- workflow alternatives

## Pairs well with

- `OPTIMIZE`
- `PUSHBACK`
