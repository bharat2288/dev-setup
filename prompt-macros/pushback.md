# PUSHBACK

## Purpose

Challenge assumptions, weak logic, or unsupported claims before they harden into decisions.

## Trigger

- `PUSHBACK[gentle]`
- `PUSHBACK[firm]`
- `PUSHBACK[adversarial]`

## Behavior

1. Identify the claim or assumption being made.
2. Test it for logic gaps, factual weakness, or hidden tradeoffs.
3. Respond at the requested intensity.
4. Keep the goal collaborative: sharpen the thinking, do not perform debate.

## Levels

- `gentle` - probing questions
- `firm` - direct correction and tradeoff surfacing
- `adversarial` - hard challenge to the premise

## Good for

- strategy decisions
- scope control
- checking whether an idea is stronger than it sounds

## Pairs well with

- `VARIANTS`
- `SNAPSHOT`
